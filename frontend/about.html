Our Mission

NeuroClam is dedicated to making mental health monitoring accessible through cutting-edge technology. We combine facial expression analysis with EEG signal processing to provide accurate, non-invasive stress detection.

How It Works

📷

Facial Expression Analysis

Our AI analyzes micro-expressions and facial muscle movements to detect stress indicators in real-time.

🧠

EEG Signal Processing

We monitor brainwave patterns (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Theta, Delta) to assess cognitive stress levels.

🕶️

VR Therapy

Immersive virtual reality experiences help users practice relaxation techniques in controlled environments.
